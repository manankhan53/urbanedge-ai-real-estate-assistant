# UrbanEdge AI Real Estate Assistant

An AI-powered real estate lead qualification and business automation system designed for buyers, sellers, and investors.

UrbanEdge combines conversational AI, workflow automation, CRM integration, and a deployed real estate website to demonstrate how repetitive lead-management processes can be automated into a structured business workflow.

## Live Demo

[Visit the Live UrbanEdge Website](https://urbanedge-ai-properties.netlify.app/)

## Project Overview

UrbanEdge was designed as a realistic real estate business automation solution.

The system allows website visitors to interact with an AI assistant, identify their purpose, provide relevant information, and enter a structured lead-qualification workflow.

The project demonstrates the integration of:

- Conversational AI
- Lead qualification
- Workflow automation
- CRM integration
- Web development
- Cloud deployment

## Business Problem

Real estate businesses often receive leads through websites and other digital channels but may still rely heavily on manual processes for:

- Collecting lead information
- Identifying buyer, seller, or investor intent
- Organizing customer information
- Routing qualified leads
- Maintaining CRM records
- Managing follow-up opportunities

UrbanEdge was designed to demonstrate how these processes can be connected through an automated AI workflow.

## AI Lead Qualification

## Seller Flow

Qualified lead information is structured and stored in Airtable for business follow-up.
