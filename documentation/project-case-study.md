# UrbanEdge AI Real Estate Assistant — Case Study

## Project Overview

UrbanEdge AI Real Estate Assistant is an AI automation system designed for real estate businesses to automate lead qualification and customer interaction.

The system allows buyers, sellers, and investors to interact with an AI assistant that collects relevant information, qualifies leads, and automatically stores structured lead data in a CRM system.

## Business Problem

Real estate businesses can lose potential customers because of:

- Delayed lead response
- Manual lead collection
- Unorganized customer information
- Inefficient follow-up processes

UrbanEdge was designed to address these problems through conversational AI and workflow automation.

## Solution

The system combines a custom landing page, conversational AI, workflow automation, and CRM storage.

The workflow:

1. A visitor enters the UrbanEdge website.
2. The AI assistant starts a conversation.
3. The visitor selects a buyer, seller, or investor path.
4. The assistant collects relevant qualification information.
5. The information is passed through a Make.com automation workflow.
6. Structured lead information is stored in Airtable.
7. The business can use the stored information for follow-up.

## Key Features

- AI-powered conversational assistant
- Buyer qualification flow
- Seller qualification flow
- Investor qualification flow
- Automated lead routing
- Airtable CRM integration
- Make.com workflow automation
